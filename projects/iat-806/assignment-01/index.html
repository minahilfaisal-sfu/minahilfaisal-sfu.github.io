Assignment 1 - Pick a Door

Red or blue. Behind each one is a random liminal space.

How to Play

You're standing in a dark hallway in front of two doors. Click the red or blue door (or press R or B) to step through, and you'll land in a random liminal space and you'll be able to see your chances of surviving in there. Click inside the room to call for help, which might raise your odds or lower them, and press Space to go back and try another door.

The screen/lights flicker in each of the liminal spaces to add to the eerie effect. There are 3 rooms in total in this sketch, and they have randomly generated messages and survival ratings.

  • The Backrooms, Level 0
  • Office, Everyone Went Home
  • Empty Pool, After Closing
  • Playground, 3:07 AM
  • The Hallway That Doesn't End
