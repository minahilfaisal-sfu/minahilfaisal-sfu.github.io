Assignment 1 - Pick a Door

Red or blue. Behind each one is a random liminal space.

How to Play

You're standing in a dark hallway in front of two doors. Click the red or blue door (or press R or B) to step through, and you'll land in a random liminal space and you'll be able to see your chances of surviving in there. Press Space to go back and try another door.
