Task Queue

Line tasks up, plan your week and month, and watch the queue shrink.

Click any task to mark it done · your queue is saved in this browser.

Edit task

Notes

A first-in, first-out queue drawn with p5.js. New tasks join at the back, the one at the front is marked “next up”, and finishing a task sends it off the canvas while everything behind it slides forward. The bar at the top fills as the queue empties, and the list grows as tasks are added. Drag a card up or down to move it to a different place in line; the ✓ button marks it done and the pencil opens an editor.

Each task can have a priority and a deadline. Switching “Order by” to Priority or Deadline turns it into a priority queue: the tasks re-sort themselves, and “next up” becomes whatever matters most rather than whatever arrived first. Overdue tasks are flagged in pink.

The Week view lays the same tasks out by day, Monday to Sunday, with a Backlog for anything undated or due after this week. The dates follow whichever week you open it in. Dragging a card to another day changes its deadline; unfinished tasks from earlier days stay put with a late tag and their original date.

The Calendar view shows the whole month. Use the arrows to look ahead or back, hover a task to see its details, and drag it onto any day, or into the Undated tray to clear its deadline.
